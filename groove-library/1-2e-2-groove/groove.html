1-2e-2 Groove

The basic groove is a three note pattern.

This groove has been called the 2+ Groove Creator by James Eager in Bass Line Mastery. It is very common.

In Wicked Game by Chris Isaak, the groove the 2e is accented and the 2 is a dotted quarter note.

Songs featuring the groove

  • Chris Isaak - Wicked Game
  • John Mayer - New Light
  • Soul Asylum - Runaway Train
